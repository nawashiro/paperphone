Paper Phone adapts selected water, park, building and minor-road palette/width
values for Cairo vector printing. It does not bundle style JSON, tile data,
sprites, Maki icons, Roboto or Mapbox fonts. It does not reproduce the engine's
pixel output. Alpha values are composited onto a paper background; widths are
adapted from CSS pixels to print points and the other road hierarchy is original.
